Dimitrios Sargiotis

Dr Sargiotis is an academic and computer scientist working on artificial intelligence and data governance for the infrastructure of cities. His work asks how cities can hand consequential decisions to computational systems without losing resilience, accountability or fairness, and is released as reproducible open-source software alongside a programme of Springer monographs.

Research statementSoftware and data

Research

AI, data governance and resilient urban systems, studied through open-source software and three book series.

Cities now hand consequential decisions to software: how traffic is routed and energy dispatched, which bridges and water pipes are repaired before they fail, which neighbourhoods are warned first of a flood, who receives scarce health and public services. His work asks how such systems can be made resilient, accountable and fair, joining forecasting, reinforcement learning and digital twins to the governance of the data on which they depend.
