Data Governance

A Guide

A practitioner's handbook for establishing and operating data governance within an organisation. It sets out frameworks, roles, policies and performance measures, and situates them within regulatory obligations and emerging technologies.

Topics

  • Governance frameworks and maturity models
  • Data quality management
  • Security, privacy and regulatory compliance
  • Stewardship, ownership and roles
  • KPIs and change management
  • AI, machine learning and blockchain

MATLAB for Civil Engineers

From Basics to Advanced Applications

A comprehensive textbook that takes civil engineers from first principles of MATLAB to the solution of realistic engineering problems. It teaches through worked examples, case studies and exercises across the principal branches of the discipline, with 256 colour illustrations.

Topics

  • MATLAB syntax and matrix computation
  • Numerical methods
  • Optimisation
  • Data visualisation
  • Structural and geotechnical applications
  • Hydrological and transportation applications

Empathy in Algorithms

Shaping Ethical AI for a Just and Inclusive Society

Argues that AI in public governance is not neutral and introduces algorithmic empathy: building fairness, privacy, transparency and accountability into AI across the whole policy cycle, from agenda-setting to evaluation. Its nine chapters formulate models for fair public-service allocation, data security, the digital divide and bias mitigation, always under human and democratic oversight.

Topics

  • AI across the public policy cycle
  • Labour displacement and reskilling
  • Fairness-constrained public service allocation
  • Privacy and security of public-sector AI
  • Digital divide and smart city inclusion
  • Bias mitigation and multi-stakeholder accountability

Blueprints for Future Living

AI-Driven Urban Innovation and Resilient Cities

Starts from documented smart-city initiatives and, for each, formulates an AI model of how the city could learn and adapt: traffic and transit in Barcelona and Singapore, energy in Amsterdam and Stockholm, waste in New York and San Francisco, and digital twins in Dubai, Herrenberg, West Cambridge, Málaga and Ålesund. It also compares how five UK cities evaluate smart-city programmes and turns to digital mental health and climate resilience.

Topics

  • Comparative smart-city case studies
  • Reinforcement-learning traffic and energy control
  • AI-enabled urban digital twins
  • Smart-city evaluation in five UK cities
  • Digital mental health and personalisation
  • Global networks for climate resilience

AI with Integrity

Shaping Ethical, Transparent, and Equitable Urban Futures

Treats fairness, transparency, accountability, privacy and sustainability as design requirements of urban AI, written into the objectives of the systems themselves. Its seven chapters formulate fairness-constrained reinforcement learning, adversarial debiasing, explainable and participatory AI, and federated, privacy-preserving data governance, and stress that such mechanisms gain legitimacy only within law and democratic oversight.

Topics

  • Algorithmic bias in urban services
  • Fairness-constrained reinforcement learning
  • Explainable and participatory urban AI
  • Privacy-preserving urban data governance
  • Accountability and compliance auditing
  • Ethical AI surveillance and human rights

Urban Harmony

AI-Enhanced Cities for Holistic Health and Well-Being

Treats physical, mental and social well-being as an explicit, measurable objective of city design. Across twelve chapters it formulates AI models for fair access to green space, walkable and cyclable streets, healthy buildings, public transport, mental-health services and participatory planning, and reviews published experience from Copenhagen, Singapore and New York.

Topics

  • Equitable green space allocation
  • Wellness modelling and urban health monitoring
  • Healthy buildings and indoor air quality
  • Walking, cycling and shared mobility
  • Privacy-preserving mental-health AI
  • Human-centred AI governance

Building Resilient Cities

AI and Civil Engineering for a Sustainable Tomorrow

Formulates AI frameworks for sustainable civil engineering across the infrastructure lifecycle, from planning and construction to maintenance, energy and water. It sets out risk-aware reinforcement learning, graph-based digital twins and multi-criteria decision models, and argues that ethical, inclusive governance must be designed in from the outset.

Topics

  • Risk-aware reinforcement learning for resilience
  • Graph neural network digital twins
  • Sustainable construction and materials
  • Renewable energy and water systems
  • Multi-criteria decisions for sustainable projects
  • Fair and privacy-preserving urban governance

City of the Future

Data-Driven Urban Design and AI Innovation

Reframes urban planning as a continuous, data-driven decision process in which interventions can be simulated, justified and revised. Its ten chapters develop hybrid models for land use and urban growth, resource and energy demand, climate risk, equitable infrastructure allocation and AI-augmented digital twins, and extend them to urban health, pollution and public services.

Topics

  • Land-use and urban growth modelling
  • Resource and energy demand forecasting
  • Climate risk and resilience planning
  • Equity-aware infrastructure allocation
  • AI-augmented urban digital twins
  • Urban health, pollution and public services

AI, Robotics, and 3D Printing

Constructing the Sustainable City of Tomorrow

Examines how artificial intelligence, robotics, digital twins and additive manufacturing are changing construction practice. Its three chapters cover construction automation, AI-driven 3D printing of modular infrastructure and AI-supported project management, and argue that these tools deliver safer and less wasteful building only inside validated, human-supervised and regulated workflows.

Topics

  • Robotics and construction site automation
  • Safety monitoring and quality inspection
  • AI-guided 3D printing and materials
  • Modular and circular additive construction
  • Adaptive scheduling and risk management
  • Adoption barriers, permitting and certification

AI for Resilient Cities

Predicting, Protecting, and Thriving in a Changing Climate

Follows the whole disaster lifecycle, from forecasting and early warning for floods, earthquakes and hurricanes to structural health monitoring, adaptive urban design and emergency response. It then extends resilience from the single city to networks of cities that share knowledge through privacy-preserving, fairness-constrained federated learning.

Topics

  • Disaster forecasting and early warning
  • Structural health monitoring and maintenance
  • Digital twins for infrastructure
  • Climate-ready urban design
  • Reinforcement learning for emergency response
  • Federated learning across city networks

Climate, Ecosystems, and Sustainable Urban Futures

Topics

  • Urban farming and food supply chains

  • Wildlife and ecosystem protection
  • Energy-aware Green AI

The Intelligent Metropolis

Redefining Urban Life through AI and Personalized Experiences

Traces the smart city from early digital urbanism to today's cyber-physical systems and compares Singapore's Smart Nation, Barcelona's citizen-centred strategy and Toronto's halted Quayside project. It then formulates AI and IoT frameworks for traffic, energy, waste, carbon reduction and personalised services, and argues that a city is intelligent only when its models are governed for fairness, privacy and public trust.

Topics

  • Evolution of the smart city
  • Singapore, Barcelona and Toronto compared
  • Traffic and vehicular networks
  • Smart grids and renewable integration
  • Urban carbon reduction
  • Personalised, privacy-aware urban services
